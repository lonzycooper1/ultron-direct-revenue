## How Wang started and built value

Wang had substantial technical preparation: engineering work including Quora and studies at MIT preceded Scale. In 2016, aged 19, he cofounded Scale with Lucy Guo and participated in Y Combinator. Profiles differ on whether they call the cohort spring or summer; YC identifies Scale as S16. Original ideas changed. His founder interview identifies the hard problem: acquiring useful labeled data was preventing developers from building AI applications.

The business made human-assisted data work accessible through a developer interface. Scale's 2017 announcement describes a $4.5M Series A, multiple APIs and millions of monthly requests. Its stated operating priorities included cost, speed, quality and customer experience. Early self-driving demand provided a narrow entry market. Wang's YC interview excerpt describes a quick landing-page launch, working directly with incoming engineers and subsequently focusing on autonomous vehicles. Distribution and customer conversations preceded a broad platform.

Later expansion followed real changes in the market: language-model data, human feedback, evaluations and enterprise/government applications. Scale's 2024 announcement records a $1B primary/secondary financing at $13.8B valuation. The 2025 company statement describes Meta's investment at over $29B valuation, Wang's move to Meta and liquidity for shareholders and vested holders. A financing valuation is not company revenue, cash profit or the founder's bank balance. The operating model also had labor, payment and quality challenges reported by WSJ; ULTRON should adopt paid accountable review, not assume unattended automation is reliable.

## Concrete adaptation shipped

- `founder-method.mjs`: sourced operating rules and an evidence-based next-action evaluator. One recurring workflow, one buyer segment, buyer discovery, small original sample, existing fixed-scope PayPal offer, defined acceptance criteria, human review and cost tracking.
- `executive-business.mjs`: guidance is added to existing queued business tasks (paid fulfillment, consented follow-ups, support and signed onboarding events). Saved task evidence and stable idempotency keys are retained; existing queued jobs are not rewritten or duplicated. Business synchronization persists the evidence snapshot. No additional model calls, cron loops or prospect messaging are introduced.
- `/founder-method`: human-readable research and implementation page. `/api/founder-method`: authenticated live evidence, recomputed from the existing order ledger. It never accepts client claims as financial evidence.
- Expansion is a review recommendation only after five accepted independent orders in the same classified segment and positive reconciled contribution profit. Five is an explicit ULTRON policy choice, not Wang's proprietary method or a statistically sufficient market guarantee. Missing cost/fee evidence, unknown niche, duplicate captures or unverified orders cannot qualify.
- Niche content is a secondary measured acquisition experiment: original posts, rights checks, truthful sponsorship disclosures, opt-in attribution and a single useful offer. Draft-only until publishing integration is verified. No fake followers, recycled stolen clips or claims of automatic wealth.

Apply to the existing audit/lead workflow first; do not open a new unrelated business or change live prices. An example pilot might map and improve one opted-in local service business's lead intake, using the existing diagnostic/intake and delivery paths. Ask the buyer for a baseline and success criteria; never invent saved hours or lost revenue. Reusable templates can accumulate permissioned lessons. Customer data is not automatically a training dataset or resale asset.
