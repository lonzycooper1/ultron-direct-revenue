## Architecture shipped
- \`ultron-video-intelligence-v39.mjs\`: 14 immutable researched signal summaries with rankings, 6 vendor-dependency layers, claim risk checks, originality and rights gates, 30-second shot list builder, bounded deduplicated persistent owner draft queue, refusal to claim publication without external evidence, arithmetic cost/contribution estimator.
- \`GET /video-intelligence\`: public-safe evidence lab, ranked opportunities, sourcing labels, dependencies, owner-token-authenticated draft form.
- \`GET /api/video-intelligence/v39\`: public-safe research and queue counts (no private drafts).
- \`GET /api/video-intelligence/v39/owner\`: private owner draft status requiring the existing Railway acquisition owner token.
- \`POST /api/video-intelligence/v39/draft\`: owner-authenticated original storyboard queue, not production or a social post.
- \`POST /api/video-intelligence/v39/preview\`: owner-authenticated storyboard preview; no recording or rendering.
- \`POST /api/video-intelligence/v39/review\`: internal verified-evidence gates; not a publisher.
- \`POST /api/video-intelligence/v39/economics\`: arithmetic scenario, no bank/PayPal access implied.
- \`ultron-video-intelligence-v39.test.mjs\`: repeatable unit and database-integrated tests executed with existing CI.

## Shortest route to the revenue goal
1. Lead with a single proof-oriented $200 pilot or existing supported PayPal diagnostic.
2. Produce original sample service videos (not copied reels) using license-checked assets; do not overinvest in video polish before demand.
3. Publish only through a connected, authorized channel and measure qualified inbound buyers; distinguish views, leads, booking, payment, fulfillment and settled net contribution.
4. Use the v39 opportunity map to improve reliability, unit economics and code QA, not inflate agent counts.
5. Make a repeatable customer success case study only after actual delivery and customer permission.

**Non-goals:** Automatic live trades, borrower applications, trusts or LLC formation, evading provider restrictions, replicating third-party footage without rights, posting to unconnected social accounts, automated owner identity verification, and unverified performance promises.
