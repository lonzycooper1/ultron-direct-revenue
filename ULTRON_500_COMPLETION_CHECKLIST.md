## Verification and safety
Each requirement is open until independently checkable evidence confirms it. Never mark requirements complete solely because software generated a plan, a provider appears connected in ChatGPT, an agent ran a loop, or a projection was calculated.
Buying domains, contacting customers beyond authorized campaigns, spending, financing, signing, real trading and fund movement require appropriate owner approval.
