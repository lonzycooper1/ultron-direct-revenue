A clearer booking routine for a small service business

Published October 4, 2026 · Product guide

When appointment details live in scattered messages, staff have to reconstruct the same information repeatedly. A written routine helps you collect the details you need and explain the next step consistently.

1. Collect the essentials

Record the requested service, preferred appointment times, contact method and relevant preparation requirements. Confirm availability before promising a slot. Collect only information needed to provide the service.

2. Confirm the appointment

Send the agreed date, time, location, service scope and cancellation terms. Ask the customer to confirm that these details are correct. Keep their reply with the appointment record.

3. Prepare reminders and follow-up

Use your customer's agreed contact channel. Review names, dates and details before sending a reminder. After the appointment, a short follow-up can ask whether anything remains unresolved. A template does not send messages or replace consent.
