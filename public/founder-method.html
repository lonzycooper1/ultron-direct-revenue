Build around a problem buyers pay to solve.

Adapted from Alexandr Wang's Scale AI journey. This is operating guidance, not a wealth forecast or an affiliation.

How the business grew

Technical preparation and founder collaboration → a developer data bottleneck → a simple human-assisted API → a narrow early market → quality, speed and cost improvements → expansion with customer demand.

Wang's ownership in Scale, financing valuations and eventual shareholder liquidity explain the fortune. The recording switches from Wang to Alexander Lari's faceless-page promotion. It does not show that Wang used that model.

Rules now used in business jobs

  1. Choose one buyer segment and one recurring expensive workflow using actual demand; no speculative mass expansion.
  2. Interview the buyer: establish current process, volume, baseline, budget owner, data rights and willingness to pay. Unknown facts remain unknown.
  3. Demonstrate a small original sample, then propose one fixed-scope paid pilot using the existing PayPal flow and existing offer prices.
  4. Before work: specify inputs, output format, exclusions, deadline and measurable buyer-approved acceptance criteria.
  5. Combine automation with independent human quality review; log source provenance, corrections, turnaround and actual delivery costs.
  6. Sell the customer outcome, not billionaire comparisons, passive-income promises or an affiliation with Wang, Scale or Meta.
  7. Record payment, delivery, acceptance, profit and settlement separately. A model response or demo proves none of these.
  8. Standardize successful work into reusable internal templates without reusing private customer data without permission.
  9. Propose recurring service only after accepted delivery; expand only after repeated accepted profitable outcomes. No automatic spending.
  10. Optional niche content: original useful posts tied to one offer, source and rights checks, truthful sponsorship disclosure, and measured opt-in conversions. Drafts only until publishing access exists.

Current evidence

Five accepted independent orders in one classified niche plus positive reconciled contribution permit an expansion review. This threshold is ULTRON's policy choice. No automatic spending or expansion follows.
