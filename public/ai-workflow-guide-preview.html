1. Choose one repetitive task

Start with a problem you can measure: duplicate customer records, slow quote triage, appointment scheduling or repeated data entry. Do not connect actual private customer information to an unapproved test tool.

3. Prototype with fictional data

Create ten dummy lead records. Validate inputs, label requests, detect duplicates and route cases to a human reviewer. Do not send real messages. Test a missed appointment, unknown question and workflow outage.

Open the fictional interactive demo

4. Review safety before launch

Check account access, confidentiality, logging, opt-outs, human escalation, system limits, licensed assets and client approval. Medical data may require a HIPAA-compliant business associate agreement. Legal information may be privileged.

5. Measure outcome, not hype

A hypothetical reduction in repetitive minutes is not necessarily saved cash. Count training, subscription costs, errors, supervision, refunds and the implementation itself. Never promise a particular return without evidence.
