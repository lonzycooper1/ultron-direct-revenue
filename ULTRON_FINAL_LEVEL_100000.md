# ULTRON Final-Level v29: 100,000 individually addressable verification checks

**Purpose:** Go beyond a list of speculative features. Turn the existing 500 evidence-gated requirements into a rigorously indexed quality and operating program.

**Count:** 500 requirements × 10 quality controls × 5 operating conditions × 4 lifecycle stages = **100,000 distinct checks**.

**Important:** These are 100,000 *test and evidence specifications*, not 100,000 separately discovered missing capabilities, implemented changes, test executions, signed contracts or verified business outcomes. Avoid the false impression that generating the matrix completes the work.

## Dimensions

Controls: provenance, authorization, validation, privacy, idempotence, resilience, economics, observability, acceptance, recovery.

Conditions: nominal operation; missing permission or dependency; partial failure; replay/duplicates; hostile or malformed input.

Stages: design review; isolated regression; release gate; live production evidence.

Checks are deterministic, lazily generated, and stable by `ULQ000001` through `ULQ100000` rather than stored as 100,000 database tasks that require processing before a customer sale.

## Priority

1. An actual independent paying customer and accepted delivery.
2. Consent-based inbound lead triage; production-authorized buyer feed.
3. Authorized Gmail and HubSpot server execution; real booking.
4. Verified payment/settlement and bank reconciliation.
5. Security/recovery and measured acquisition economics.
6. Retention, distribution, partner validation, and legitimate lender-readiness documents.

**Safety:** no unauthorized bulk outreach, provider-bypass, purchases, ad spending, loans, real trading, money transfers or fabricated results.

## URLs after a successful deployment
- `/final-level`: index and operating priorities.
- `/api/final-level`: factual overview, authorization gates and latest supervisor priorities.
- `/api/final-level/checks?offset=0&limit=50`: inspect one page of checks (max 100).
- `/api/final-level/checks?requirementId=C001`: 200 checks for one requirement.
- `/api/final-level/checks/ULQ100000`: inspect a specific check.
- `/api/final-level/export.csv`: stream all 100,000 unique, human-readable checks in CSV; no fabricated pass results.

The 500 underlying primary requirements remain in `ULTRON_500_COMPLETION_CHECKLIST.md`.
