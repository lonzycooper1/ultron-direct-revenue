# Missed-lead workflow opportunity: sourced research, 2026-10-20

## Evidence and audience

Audience hypothesis: small home-service operators who receive calls while on
jobs and need reliable intake, follow-up and booking. Vendor positioning supports
this problem category, but does not prove a particular business has the problem
or is willing to purchase ULTRON. No prospects were contacted. No engagement,
traffic, customer demand, ROI or revenue metrics were available or invented.

## Practical differentiation and money filter

Do not launch a generic unverified voice agent against established providers.
Prioritize an evidence-backed workflow audit that measures the customer's current
intake, identifies handoff gaps, and recommends an appropriately scoped fix using
existing authorized tools. This is a hypothesis, not validated demand or a newly
activated offer.

Three highest-impact improvements:
1. Make intake, permission and actual response timestamps visible.
2. Bind proposal, verified payment, exact delivery and acceptance so fulfillment
   gaps are detectable without claiming revenue from a checkout link.
3. Verify one provider integration at a time against the customer's real process.

Defer more agent personas, speculative SKU counts and unsupported earnings claims.

## Acquisition experiment and content

After scoped owner publication approval, place the existing free diagnostic on
an owned channel. Invite voluntary inquiry and collect explicit follow-up
permission. Do not scrape contact details and treat them as consent. Measure
visits, completed diagnostics, consented inquiries, qualified conversations,
proposal acceptance, provider-confirmed payments and accepted delivery separately.
No campaign was sent or published during this research.

Five original content angles: measure response gaps without inventing lost
revenue; compare per-call/per-caller/per-minute costs; map the handoff from inquiry
to booking; distinguish a paid order from completed delivery; choose the smallest
necessary integration before buying another platform.

## Economics and time to first sale

Actual labor hours, provider usage, acquisition cost, fees and demand are unknown.
Do not produce a profit or time-to-sale estimate from these missing inputs.
Scenario worksheet: agreed price minus observed processor fees, measured labor
cost, compute/media/telephony use, support and refunds. Settlement stays separate.
A seven-day experiment is a measurement window, not a promise of a sale in seven
days. Stop or revise if no qualified demand appears.
